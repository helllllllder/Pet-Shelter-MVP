Pet Shelter App User Guide

Complete operator reference and walkthrough for managing animals, medical care, categorized inventory, facility maintenance, and communications with zero cloud dependency.

FR01 / TC-FR01

1. Local Operator Profile & Authentication

The app operates in a zero-network environment. Your operator profile binds locally to your device installation.

Initial Setup 👤
On first launch, submit your Full Name and Email. The app issues a local UUIDv7 identifier and stores it in secure device storage.
Returning Auto-Login ⚡
Subsequent application opens automatically recognize your local device profile, bypassing login prompts.
FR02, FR04 / NFR08

2. Multi-Shelter Management & Context Isolation

Manage distinct rescue organizations or shelter branches from a single device without data leakage.

  • Create Facilities
    Add one or more shelters. Shelters are allowed to have duplicate names without namespace collisions.
  • Switch Context Instantly
    Switch between facilities via the top dropdown. All active queries, caches, and repository bindings immediately re-scope to the chosen shelter.
  • Dirty State Protection
    If you switch shelters while editing an animal profile or care record, a modal warns you to save or discard changes before leaving.
FR05–FR09

FR19 / TC-FR19

7. Categorized Inventory Management

Keep track of food, pharmaceuticals, and shelter supplies with precision units of measure.

🥫 Food
Kibble, wet food, puppy formula, veterinary diets (measured in KG, G, UNITS).
💊 Medication
Antibiotics, vaccines, pain relievers, dewormers (measured in ML, DOSES, UNITS).
🧼 Cleaning
Disinfectants, bleach, kennel wash, detergent (measured in L, ML, UNITS).
🩺 Equipment
Syringes, bandages, microchip scanners, gloves (measured in UNITS, BOXES).
FR20, FR21 / ADR-0004

8. 1-Click Usage Templates & Alert Rules

⚡ 1-Click Care Event Stock Decrement

Bundle supplies into templates (e.g. "Standard Puppy Vaccine Pack" = 1 Vaccine Vial + 1 Syringe). When administering care, selecting the template atomically decrements all linked stock in SQLite.

Low-Stock Alert Rule 📉
Triggers immediately when current stock drops below your configured threshold (e.g. ≤ 10 units).
Expiration Proximity Rule ⏳
Triggers a warning banner when items are within $N$ days of expiring (e.g. within 30 days).
FR24, FR25 / ADR-0004

9. Notifications & Retry Escalation Policy

Tier Channel Delivery Behavior
Standard Tier In-App Banner Instant delivery (<5s) for care due dates, inventory alerts, and overdue tasks.
Custom Tier Email / SMS / Push Queued with a 3-Retry Policy. If delivery fails 3 times, escalates to a prominent high-visibility in-app banner with dismiss action.
